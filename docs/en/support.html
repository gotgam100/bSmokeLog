FAQ

How do I log an entry?

On the Today screen, press and hold the clock to add or delete one at that time. Press and hold the center to add one at the current time. Hold a dot briefly and drag to change its time, or use "Add manually" below to enter a time and a note.

Can I log without opening the app?

Yes — from the Home Screen widget, Lock Screen widgets, the Control Center button and Shortcuts (Siri). On iPhone 15 Pro and later you can also assign it to the Action button.

The widget count doesn't update right away.

iOS decides when widgets refresh. It usually catches up within a few minutes, and opening the app updates it immediately.

I want to leave a trip or an unusual day out of my stats.

Press and hold that date in the calendar and choose "Exclude from totals". The entries stay, but the day is left out of averages, totals and charts. Use the same menu to include it again.

How do I turn on iCloud sync?

Settings → Data → iCloud sync, then relaunch the app. Devices signed in to the same Apple Account will stay in sync. Your device must be signed in to iCloud.

Can I export my data?

Settings → Data → "Export as CSV" creates a file with date, time, type and note that opens in Excel or Numbers.

How do I delete everything?

Settings → Data → "Delete all entries". This can't be undone, so export a CSV first if you need a copy. Deleting the app also removes its on-device data.
