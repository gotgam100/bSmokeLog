Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

B Smoke Log (B흡연로그) values your privacy and complies with applicable laws. The app works without an account or sign-in, and it never sends your data to any server operated by the developer.

1. Data We Collect

  • We do not collect identifiers such as your name, email address or phone number.
  • What you enter yourself — smoking entries (time, type, note), your types, the daily threshold and days excluded from totals — is stored on your device.
  • Settings such as language, haptics, week start and whether notifications are on are stored on your device.
  • We use no analytics, advertising identifiers or crash-reporting tools.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Storing your entries and showing them in the calendar, the 24-hour clock and statistics
  • Logging an entry and showing today's count from widgets, Control Center and Shortcuts
  • Sending a weekly summary notification, only if you turn it on
  • Restoring your settings when you reopen the app

3. iCloud Sync

  • If you turn on iCloud sync in Settings, your entries and types are stored in the private CloudKit database of your own Apple Account and synced to your other devices signed in to the same account.
  • That data is protected by Apple's iCloud terms and privacy policy. The developer cannot access or read it.
  • You can turn iCloud sync off at any time in Settings.

4. Retention and Deletion

  • On-device data is removed when you delete the app. You can also use "Delete all entries" inside the app.
  • Data stored in iCloud can be deleted from iPhone Settings under iCloud storage management.
  • "Export as CSV" creates a file only when you run it, and the file goes only to the destination you choose in the share sheet.

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share your information with third parties, except when required by law.

6. Permissions and Platform Features

  • Notifications: requested only when you turn on the weekly summary, and used to deliver it on Sunday evenings.
  • iCloud (CloudKit): used to sync between your devices when you turn sync on.
  • Widgets, Control Center and Shortcuts (App Intents): read and write the same on-device data so you can log an entry and see today's count without opening the app.

7. Children's Privacy

B Smoke Log is intended for adults. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 14.
